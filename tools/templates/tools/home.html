What Is an ATS Score?

Most companies run resumes through an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) before a human ever reads them. Your ATS Score estimates how well your resume will survive that first automated pass.

  • Standard section headers (Experience, Education, Skills) are detected correctly
  • Keywords are checked against the job description you're targeting
  • Dense paragraphs or missing bullet points get flagged
  • Tables, images, or multi-column layouts that ATS software can't parse are called out

A score above 80 means your resume is in great shape for most ATS systems. Below that, it's worth tightening up formatting or adding a few missing keywords before you apply.
