About Us

Smarter Resumes Better Opportunities !

ResumeIQ is a free toolkit that helps you build, score, and tailor your resume — powered by a scoring engine built right into this app, with nothing sent to a third-party AI.

Why We Created ResumeIQ

Most resumes never make it past the ATS bots that screen them before a human ever looks. We built ResumeIQ to close that gap — giving every job seeker free, instant access to the same signals recruiters and applicant tracking systems look for, without the guesswork.

Resume Optimizer

Making Resume Building Easier

Every tool runs on a lightweight Python scoring engine built directly into this Django app — parsing your resume, detecting sections, matching keywords against job descriptions, and scoring readability, all processed locally and never handed off to a third party. No sign-up required, no waiting on an external API — just instant, honest feedback so you can walk into your next application with confidence.
