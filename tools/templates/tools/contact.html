Quick Answers

What is ResumeIQ?

ResumeIQ is a free toolkit that helps you create, analyze, and optimize your resume — all processed locally with nothing sent to a third-party AI.

How does Resume Analyzer work?

Upload your resume and our built-in scoring engine parses each section, checking structure, keywords, and readability to give you an instant score.

What is ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software recruiters use to scan and filter resumes before a human ever reviews them. ResumeIQ helps your resume get past that first screen.

How can I create a resume?

Head to the Resume Creator tool and fill in your details through the guided form — it generates a clean, ATS-friendly resume for you.
